Info

Stretcher is an exhibition project and more, founded in 2023 by the artists Karin Borer and Daniel Kurth. The project is part of the collectively run InfoSpace in Basel, Switzerland.

Opening hours during exhibitions: Monday / Saturday 13:00 to 18:00. And by appointment.

Find us here on the map. From Basel SBB → Tram 10, Tram 11, Train S3 to Dreispitz. From Basel Bad Bhf → Bus 36 to St. Jakob. From city center → Tram 14, Tram 10, Tram 11 to St. Jakob.
